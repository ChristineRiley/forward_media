# Sunday ProPresenter Seat Outline

This is designed to be a checklist/guide to running the presentation computer, TV's, projector and
lights for Sunday services.

## Pre-Service

### 7:00am: Setup

1. Ensure media computer is on. It is a good idea to reboot it at this time too. Do not install any updates.
1. While the computer is rebooting, it is a good idea to make sure the blue stage lights are on.
2. Turn on both TVs and the projector.
1. Make sure the power strip is turned on that runs the rail lights in the booth.
1. Turn on the center and side lights for practice.
1. Make sure the volume is all the way up on the computer
2. Check that the Bible slides are configured correctly.

### During practice

The songs should already be loaded in the correct section in ProPres.

1. Pay attention to the practice and make sure the song order is correct. Adjust the arrangement as
   needed.
1. Ensure the lyrics are formatted properly.
   - 1920 x 1080 screen size
   - No more than 4 lines per slide
   - Font size > 80pt < 100pt
   - Try to ensure line breaks make sense with song
   - Use groups! (Verse 1, Chorus, Verse 2, Bridge, etc) These are available in the arrangement editor.
   - The first slide should be in the intro group with no background, a clear all queue, and Worship
     screen set.

### Between Practice and Service

1. Setup teaching slides from Dropbox
1. Get any clarification needed from Worship team
2. 16 minitues before service the clock reset macro runs. 15 mins before services the countdown clock begins. Check the multiview or the projector display to ensure it is running.
1. **15m before Service**: Turn off center lights
1. **15m before Service**: Ensure the pre-service slide deck has started (announcement loop). Make sure to execute the Pre-Service screen control macro. This sets the projector to the appropriate display configuration with clocks.
1. **15m before Service**: Start Spotify pre-service playlist
1. **5m before Service**: Turn off side lights

### 9:00 and 11:00am: Service Start

1. On the hour the service duration timer will automatically begin. Ensure it is running.
2. Pay attention to worship leader and be prepared to turn on spot lights when they give a thumbs up.
1. Click the intro slide on the first song to clear the pre-service loop and setup Worship stage
   display.
1. Follow Worship with lyrics.
1. As worship is ending, get ready for MC + Announcements
   - *Ensure Spotify is paused/stopped* - [F8 pauses media]

### ~9:30 and 11:30: MC and Announcements

1. Be prepared for testimonies. [ctr+b] launches the bible, try to pull up any verses used. Default
   to NKJV.
1. **IMPORTANT**: Turn on side lights when band is dismissed.
1. Wait for the MC to say roll the announcements, look to the sound person to ensure they are ready.
   When they give the thumbs up, start the video!
1. As soon as they start, *turn off the spot lights.*
2. Be prepared to switch to the sermon slides.  Select the first slide with the Forward logo or run the Teaching screen control macro to set the appropriate stage display.
1. When announcements end, *turn on the spot lights quickly followed by the side lights.*

### Teaching

1. Pay attention and be ready to assist Clint or any other teacher.
1. In general Clint drives this section, but sometimes asks for a slide or follow along.
2. Ensure that the sound person has the media channel muted.
3. When Clint activates the PADs, start the end of service playlist in Spotify.

### 10:28am and 12:28pm: End of service

1. Start end service slide loop.
2. Run the Post Service Macro.  This sets the proper stage display configuraiton with no clocks.
